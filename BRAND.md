# The logoless mark

Files are in `public/brand/`. Earlier versions are kept in `v1` to `v6`. `alt-no-holes/` has a spare version with no hollow anywhere (the play button becomes an open notch), though it reads less like an "o".

## What it is

Two bold letters, **L** and **o**, built to fill one perfect square: the first two letters of **lOgoless**. The silhouette is exactly 1:1, so the mark is its own app icon and needs no box around it.

## What it means

1. **"Lo" is where the name begins.** L for logo, o for the hole in the middle of it. The mark is the start of the word: logoless.
2. **The hole in the "o" is a play button.** An "o" is a ring around an empty middle. Here the empty middle is the clip. Where a logo would normally sit there is nothing, and what shows through is a play button. It is cut clean through, so it always shows whatever is behind the mark.
3. **It is the only hollow in the mark.** The L is solid. The rim of the "o" is solid. One emptiness, and it is the point of the name: logo-**less**.
4. **The play button points right.** Forward, onward, the way views and earnings travel.
5. **Clipped on top, square on the bottom.** The tops of both letters are cut at exactly 45 degrees, like a clip trimmed to length: the L's top-left corner and both top corners of the "o". Their bottoms are square and stand on one flat baseline, so the mark sits down firmly in its frame. Nothing is round except the tiny softening on each corner.
6. **Same height, same baseline.** Clippers, brands and viewers are equals. Neither letter is taller or louder than the other.
7. **A square, filled completely.** Nothing sticks out. The mark fills its frame the way a clip fills a screen.
8. **The "o" is the wide one.** The L is a slim, tall column and the "o" gets the room, because the "o" is where the clip plays.

## Rules

- Colours: `#f3f2ee` (paper) on `#0b0b0c` (ink). The light version swaps them. A single red `#ff2a2a` may replace paper for promotions.
- Never stretch, rotate, outline, shadow or glow the mark. Never fill the play button.
- Keep clear space around the mark of at least the width of the L's stem (about a third of the mark).
- For tiny sizes (favicons) use `favicon-*.png`, which scale the mark up inside the square.
- In app icons the mark covers 57% of the square, which keeps it fully visible inside round and rounded Android and iOS masks.
