# The logoless mark

Files are in `public/brand/`. Earlier versions are kept in `v1` to `v8`. `alt-chevron/` has a spare with a slimmer notch.

## What it is

Two bold letters of exactly the same width and height, **L** and **l**, built to fill one perfect square. Every corner is crisp and only slightly softened. The silhouette is exactly 1:1, so the mark is its own app icon and needs no box around it.

## What it means

1. **"ll", for logo-less.** Two l's, side by side, equal in every way. One is the L, with a foot. The other is plain and tall. Together they are the name.
2. **The gap is the "less".** The narrow channel between the two letters runs from the top edge of the mark to the bottom edge, and halfway down it swells into a play button. The empty space is the point of the name: where a logo would sit there is nothing, and what the empty space makes is a clip.
3. **Nothing is closed in.** The play button is cut in from the edge, not punched out of the middle. No part of the mark is hollow or hidden. The emptiness is open to the outside, so it always shows whatever is behind the mark.
4. **The foot of the L points at it.** The clipped end of the foot leans toward the play button. The path of a clip runs from the person who made it, along the foot of the L, into the play button, and on to the views it earns.
5. **Clipped at 45 degrees.** The top-left corner of both letters is cut at exactly 45 degrees, like a clip trimmed to length. The end of the foot is trimmed at the same angle.
6. **Square.** Every corner is a clean right angle or a clean 45-degree cut, each softened by the same small amount, like a well-worn tile. The bottoms are flat and stand on one baseline.
7. **Every width is the same.** The letters are equally wide, the L's stem is as thick as its foot, and the gap is one narrow channel. Clippers, brands and viewers are equals. Nothing is bigger than anything else.
8. **A square, filled completely.** Nothing sticks out. The mark fills its frame the way a clip fills a screen.

## Rules

- Colours: `#f3f2ee` (paper) on `#0b0b0c` (ink). The light version swaps them. A single red `#ff2a2a` may replace paper for promotions.
- Never stretch, rotate, outline, shadow or glow the mark. Never fill in the play button.
- Keep clear space around the mark of at least the thickness of the L's stem (about a quarter of the mark).
- For tiny sizes (favicons) use `favicon-*.png`, which scale the mark up inside the square.
- In app icons the mark covers 57% of the square, which keeps it fully visible inside round and rounded Android and iOS masks.
