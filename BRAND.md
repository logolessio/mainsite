# The logoless mark

Files are in `public/brand/` (`v1`, `v2` hold the earlier versions).

## What it means

The mark is two letters, **L** and **l**: "logo-less". Every part of it carries an idea.

1. **The play button cut out of the second letter.** logoless is the place where views have value, with no logo in the way. A logo would normally sit in that space. Here the space is empty, and the thing left in it is the clip: a play button, punched all the way through so you can see the page behind it. "Less" is literally the hole.
2. **The L's foot points at it.** The top edge of the L's foot lines up with the middle of the play button. The foot is the path a clip takes: from the person who made it, to the views it earns. Its clipped end is an arrow in miniature.
3. **Equal heights.** Both letters stand at exactly the same height, on the same baseline. Clippers, brands and viewers are treated as equals: nobody's name is bigger than anyone else's.
4. **Clipped corners.** Both letters have their top-left corner cut at exactly 45 degrees, the way a clip is trimmed. The cut is the same angle everywhere in the mark, including the end of the foot.
5. **The gap.** A narrow channel separates the two letters. It is the "less" between "logo" and "less", and it keeps the foot and the card from touching.

## Rules

- Colours: `#f3f2ee` (paper) on `#0b0b0c` (ink). The light version swaps them. A single red `#ff2a2a` may replace paper for promotions.
- Never stretch, outline, add shadows or a glow. Never recolour the play window: it must always show whatever is behind the mark.
- Keep clear space around the mark of at least the width of the L's stem.
- For tiny sizes (favicons) use `favicon-*.png`, which are scaled up inside the square.
